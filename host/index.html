Subvocal Radar

60 GHz radar dongle: watch jaw and lip motion while you silently mouth words, and record labelled takes for training.

Microphone input (PC, Android, iPhone)

The dongle appears as a USB microphone named "Radar Microphone". Its sound is a 2 kHz carrier that holds the radar signal; this page decodes it.

Phasor (I/Q)

A moving surface traces an arc; one full turn is 2.45 mm of motion at 61 GHz.
